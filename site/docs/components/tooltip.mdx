## Examples

### Default

A tooltip only adds a hint: its trigger keeps its own label. An [Icon button](/components/icon-button) shows its `label` as one already. Mount a `TooltipProvider` around the app, so moving from one trigger to the next shows its tip without waiting again.

<Example name="tooltip/demo" />

### Sides

`side` opens it on the `top`, the default, or on the `right`, `bottom`, or `left`.

<Example name="tooltip/sides" />

### With a shortcut

`shortcut`, written as `Mod E`, shows after the text, with `Mod` as ⌘ on a Mac and Ctrl elsewhere.

<Example name="tooltip/shortcut" />

## Anatomy

Each part's `data-slot`, which a theme styles, is beside it.

```tsx
<TooltipProvider>
  <Tooltip>
    <TooltipTrigger /> // tooltip-trigger, or the slot of the element it renders
    <TooltipContent shortcut="Mod E" /> // tooltip-content, with a kbd and a tooltip-arrow
  </Tooltip>
</TooltipProvider>
```
