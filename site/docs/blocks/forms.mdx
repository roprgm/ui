Fields, buttons, selects, and toggles side by side, in a card and on the page, to see how their sizes and paddings sit together.

## What to look for

- An `Input` and a `Select` are as tall as the `Button` beside them.
- On a card, controls take the card's colors, so they keep the same contrast as on the page.
- A card pads its content as one section until it holds `Section`s, which take over.
