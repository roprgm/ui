OpenLight's layout from the library alone. Try the Brush tool, and drag the panel's edge to resize it.

## How it's built

- The tool rail is a column of segmented icon `Tabs`, each with its name and key as a tooltip.
- With the Brush tool, a bar floats over the canvas: the brush size as a toolbar `Slider`, an `Overlay` `Chip`, and a `Popover` with more.
- The panel is `material-panel` with `sections()` on a plain element, so its `Section`s stack with a line between each, as in a card.
- Its layers are `ListItem`s, and its adjustments `Slider`s.
