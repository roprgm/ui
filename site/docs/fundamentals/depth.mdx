A card sets the colors of what sits on it, so its controls keep their contrast: a field still sits below its ground, and a control still stands above it.

## Raised popups

A popup that opens over a card takes `raised`, and stands a level more. `Select` and `Combobox` take it too.

```tsx
<PopoverContent raised>…</PopoverContent>
```

## Containers

`material-card`, `material-panel`, and `material-float` each set the materials inside them. In the default theme, a field sits two levels below what it's on, and a container lifts what sits on it two levels. One on a card stands as a raised popup does, with no deeper nesting. See [Materials](/fundamentals/materials).

```tsx
<div className="rounded-xl p-5 material-card">…</div>
```
