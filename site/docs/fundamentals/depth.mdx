A card sets the colors of what sits on it, so its controls keep their contrast: a field still sits below its ground, and a control still stands above it.

## Raised popups

A popup that opens over a card takes `raised`, and stands a level more. `Select` and `Combobox` take it too.

```tsx
<PopoverContent raised>…</PopoverContent>
```

## Regions

`surface-card`, `surface-panel`, and `surface-float` each paint their background and set the colors of what sits on them. In the default theme, a field sits two levels below what it's on, and a card, a panel, or what floats lifts what sits on it two levels, with no deeper nesting.

```tsx
<div className="rounded-xl p-5 surface-card">…</div>
```
