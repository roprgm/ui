## Principles

- **Parts, not props.** A component's parts come as children, and an overlay's trigger renders your own element.
- **Materials, not shadows.** A component is made of a material, such as `material-control` or `material-card`, and the theme draws it.
- **Containers set their materials.** A card, a panel, or a popover sets how the materials inside it look, so a control keeps its contrast anywhere, and any class of yours can do the same.
- **CSS before JavaScript.** Native elements and Tailwind variants first; Base UI for positioning, focus, and typeahead.
