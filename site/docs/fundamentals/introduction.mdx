## Principles

- **Parts, not props.** A component's parts come as children, and an overlay's trigger renders your own element.
- **Colors follow the region.** A control takes its fill from the surface it sits on, so it keeps its contrast on the page, on a card, or in a popover.
- **Surfaces, not shadows.** A box says what it is, such as `surface-control` or `surface-card`, and the theme draws it.
- **CSS before JavaScript.** Native elements and Tailwind variants first; Base UI for positioning, focus, and typeahead.
