## Tokens

`--spacing-control-sm`, `--spacing-control`, and `--spacing-control-lg` are the three heights; only controls take them, so a theme scales them alone. A component reads them as `h-(--spacing-control)`, so a `className` such as `h-8` replaces the height.

```tsx
<Button size="sm">Crop</Button>
```

## Corners

Corners take Tailwind's scale: `rounded-md` for controls, `rounded-lg` for lists, and `rounded-xl` for cards and popups. Nested corners are concentric: the outer radius less the inset.
